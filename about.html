About Recipy

Our Mission

Recipy was built for people who love food but live busy lives. We believe that a great home-cooked meal should never be out of reach — even on a packed Tuesday evening.

Unlike other recipe sites that list total cooking time, Recipy always shows active prep time: the minutes you actually spend at the stove or chopping board. A slow-cooker meal might take eight hours, but if you only spend fifteen minutes preparing it, that's what matters.

Browse by dietary preference, filter by your available time, and discover recipes that genuinely fit your life — not an idealised version of it.

Key Features

  • Filter by active prep time — not total cooking time
  • Dietary preference matching — vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free
  • Nutrition at a glance — calories, protein, and key macros
  • Step-by-step instructions with technique notes
  • Media library — videos, images, and audio guides

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "active prep time" mean?

Active prep time is the number of minutes you spend actively cooking — chopping, stirring, frying. It excludes passive waiting such as oven time, marinating, or slow-cooker hours. This gives you a realistic picture of how demanding a recipe actually is on your evening.

How do dietary filters work?

Each recipe is tagged with one or more dietary labels (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free). When you select a dietary preference in the filter form, only recipes matching that label are shown. Selecting "Any" removes the filter and shows all recipes.

Are the recipes updated regularly?

Yes. New recipes are added on a rolling basis. You can subscribe with your email address on the Recipes page to receive a notification whenever a new recipe matching your preferences is published.
